Privacy Policy

HB Cure · Last updated: May 2026

No Personal Data Collected

HB Cure does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account or provide any identifying information.

No Tracking

HB Cure does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any other tracking technology. Your usage of the app is entirely private.

Local Bluetooth Communication Only

All communication between HB Cure and compatible CureClip devices occurs locally via Bluetooth. No data is sent to external servers or third parties at any time.

No Data Sharing

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with or sold to any third party.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the App Store listing.
